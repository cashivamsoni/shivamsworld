💡 What I Do

Four things I spend most of my time on - and genuinely enjoy.


Front-End Development

Self-taught and self-driven, I build and maintain multiple websites end-to-end - design, code, and deployment. I love solving everyday problems with code, and I'm building toward reusing my own codebase for ERP and automation tools once I'm articled as a CA.

Calligraphy & Design

Self-taught since 2022, calligraphy is where my patience and precision come together. It's often the first thing people compliment when they meet me - and one of the most satisfying things I make with my hands.

Content Creation

I run Shivam's World on YouTube, turning DIYs, sketches, and craft projects into videos people can follow along with - from concept and filming to editing and publishing.

Numbers & Finance

On the academic side, I'm working through CA Intermediate alongside my BCom, building the technical foundation for a career in accounting and finance - with Deloitte, Lucknow, as a goal after qualifying.

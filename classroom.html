Frequently Asked Questions

Are the classes free?

Yes. Live classes, recordings and all PDF notes on this page are free for every student.

When are the live classes?

The next class and its countdown are shown at the top of the Live Class section. Subscribe and turn on the bell on YouTube to get notified.

I missed the live class. What now?

Every class is saved on the channel. Open the Class Recordings section and pick up where you left off.

Why is the stream quality kept light?

Streams are kept lightweight so they run smoothly on mobile data. The PDF notes carry all the formats and details in full clarity.

Which classes does this cover?

Class 12, B.Com and BBA topics, with CA Foundation added wherever it is relevant, plus visual revision charts for CA Inter.

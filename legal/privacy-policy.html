Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Shivam's World collects when you browse the site, use the Store, sign in, place an order, write a review, ask for a return or chat with our assistant, and how we use and protect it. By using the site you agree to this policy.

In short: we only collect what is needed to make and deliver your order and to look after it afterwards. We never sell your data, we never see your card or UPI PIN, and you can ask us to correct or delete your information at any time.

1. Who we are

Shivam's World is a YouTube channel, creative brand and handmade-gifts store run by Shivam Soni from Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh, India. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” mean Shivam's World, and the website is shivams-world.vercel.app.

2. Information we collect

Depending on what you do on the site, we collect the following:

  • Sign-in details. You sign in to the Store with your Google account. We receive your name, email address and a unique account ID from Google. We never see your Google password.
  • Order details. Your name, phone number, delivery address, an optional note, the items and amounts, any coupon used, the 12-digit UPI transaction ID (UTR) you enter after paying, and the order status and time.
  • Your location. If you pin your location on the map or tap “use my location”, we receive the latitude and longitude you choose. See section 4.
  • Saved details. If you save them in My account, your name, phone number, address and optional location, so you do not have to type them again.
  • Reward points. Your points balance, and the points used or earned on each order.
  • Reviews. Your star rating and optional comment, together with your first name and the initial of your last name, and the order and product it belongs to.
  • Return requests. The order, the reason and any details you write. For a refund we also collect your UPI ID, bank account number and IFSC code so that we can send the money.
  • WhatsApp and calls. When you message or call us, we see the details you share, such as photos, names, quotes, sizes and colours for personalised products.
  • Assistant chat. The messages you type into the AI assistant.
  • Technical data. Like most websites, our hosting and content providers can see your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. Your browser also stores small items such as your cart and light or dark mode choice (see section 8).

3. How we use your information

  • To create, confirm, make, pack, deliver and track your order, and to show you an estimated delivery date.
  • To verify your payment, issue invoices and keep our accounts.
  • To contact you about your order, for example to ask for photos or details for a personalised product, or to confirm delivery.
  • To handle returns, replacements and refunds.
  • To keep your reward points balance up to date.
  • To prevent misuse, for example to allow a coupon only once per customer and to stop fake payments.
  • To show genuine reviews, and to improve the Store and the assistant.
  • To meet our legal and tax obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising and we do not sell it.

4. Your location

  • We ask for your location only to deliver your order and to estimate the delivery date. Your browser asks for your permission first, and you can say no and place the pin by hand on the map instead.
  • Your pinned location is saved with your order so that we can find your address. If you also save it in My account, it is kept there until you remove it (use the “Remove” link) or ask us to delete it.
  • The estimated delivery date is worked out on your own device from the pin you choose, in the same way for every visitor.
  • The map is provided by OpenStreetMap. When the map loads, your browser requests map pictures from OpenStreetMap, which can see your IP address.

5. Payments

  • You pay by UPI using your own UPI app. The payment is processed by your bank and UPI app, not by this website.
  • We never receive or store your card number, UPI PIN or net-banking password.
  • We store the UTR you type in, so that we can match the payment to your order.
  • To send a refund, we need your UPI ID, bank account number and IFSC code. These are entered only in the return form, used only for that refund, and visible only to you and to us.

6. Who can see your information

Your orders, return requests and saved details are stored in a private database. You can see your own; only Shivam Soni, as the site administrator, can see everyone's. Product reviews are public and show only your first name and last initial.

We rely on trusted services to run the site. They process information on our behalf, under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase for sign-in and the database that holds your account, orders, reviews and return requests.
  • Vercel for hosting the website and the assistant's server function.
  • Google Gemini to write the assistant's replies (see section 7).
  • OpenStreetMap for the location map, Google Fonts, Font Awesome and cdnjs for fonts, icons and code libraries, and YouTube image servers for product pictures.
  • WhatsApp when you choose to message us there.
  • Rapido, which we use for deliveries within the city, and Porter, which we use for orders sent to another city. We share only the name, phone number, address and map pin needed to deliver your order, and their riders may call you on the number you gave.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, or to protect our rights and prevent fraud.

7. The AI assistant

  • When you send a message, it is sent through our server to Google's Gemini service, which writes a reply. Google handles that text under its own terms.
  • We do not save the conversation. It stays in your browser's memory only until you leave or refresh the page.
  • Please do not type bank details, UPI IDs, transaction IDs, passwords or other sensitive information into the chat.
  • The assistant can be wrong. For prices, order status or anything important, check with us on WhatsApp.

8. Cookies and browser storage

  • We do not use advertising or tracking cookies of our own.
  • Your browser keeps your cart (“sw-cart”) and your light or dark mode choice (“sw-theme”) on your device so they are remembered. Firebase also keeps your sign-in state in your browser until you sign out.
  • Other pages of the site embed YouTube videos and a visitor counter, which may set their own cookies. You can block or clear cookies and site data in your browser settings; the Store will still open, but you may need to sign in again.

9. How long we keep your information

  • Orders, invoices and payment records are kept for as long as we need them for accounting, tax and legal reasons, and to deal with any later question about your order.
  • Saved details in My account are kept until you ask us to delete them.
  • Reviews stay on the product page until you ask us to remove or change them.
  • Bank details given for a refund are used only for that refund. You can ask us to delete them once your refund has reached you.

10. How we protect it

Our database is set up so that you can read only your own orders, saved details and return requests, and everything is sent over a secure (HTTPS) connection. No website or storage system is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security, but we take reasonable care. Please keep your Google account secure and sign out on shared devices.

11. Your choices and rights

You can:

  • see your saved details and orders in My account, update your details and sign out whenever you like;
  • ask us to give you a copy of the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it;
  • withdraw your permission for us to use your information, understanding that we may then be unable to process or deliver an order, and that we may keep records the law requires us to keep;
  • turn off location sharing in your browser.

To use these rights, write to babitavrm60@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp. We will reply as soon as we reasonably can.

12. Children

The Store is meant for adults. If you are under 18, please use it only with the help and permission of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect information from children without that permission. If you think a child has given us information on their own, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when we add a feature. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. If we make an important change, we will say so on the Store. Using the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

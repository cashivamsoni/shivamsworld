6. Reward points

Shivam's World rewards your purchases with points. Here is how they work:

  • Earning. You earn 1 point for every full ₹100 you pay for the items. This is the price after any points and coupon, and the delivery charge is not counted. For example, if you pay ₹499 for the items, you earn 4 points.
  • When they arrive. Points are added to your account once your order is marked “Delivered”. You can see your balance in My account.
  • Value. 1 point = ₹1 off the price of the items. You can use points once you hold at least 20 points, by ticking “Use my points” at checkout. All the points you can use (up to the price of the items) are used in one go.
  • Order of discounts. Points are taken off the price of the items first. Any coupon is then worked out on what is left.
  • No cash value. Points cannot be exchanged for money, sold or transferred, and cannot be used against the delivery charge. They belong to the Google account you sign in with.
  • Cancelled and returned orders. If an order is cancelled, the points you used are given back and no points are earned. If a refund is sent for a return, the points you used are given back and the points earned on that order are taken off. A replacement does not change your points.
  • Fair use. We may correct mistakes and remove points that were added in error or through misuse or fraud. We may change or end the points programme; before we make a change that affects points you already hold, we will give notice on the Store.

  • Payments are handled by your bank and UPI app. We are not responsible for failures, delays or charges on their side. If money was taken but the order did not go through, message us with the UTR and we will sort it out.
  • You will receive an order ID starting with SW-. You can follow the status (payment being verified, payment confirmed, packed, shipped, delivered or cancelled) and download an invoice from My account.

10. Returns, replacements and refunds

If something is not right, you can ask for a return from the Store. Here is how it works:

  • When. Only after the order is marked “Delivered”, and within 7 days of delivery. Open My account, find the order and choose “Return or replace”.
  • Reasons. The product arrived damaged, you received the wrong item, the quality is not as expected, it is not as described or shown, or another problem that you describe. We may decline a request that does not match one of these reasons.
  • Your choice. You can ask for a refund or for a replacement. We review every request and decide in good faith. Each order can have one return request.
  • Replacement. We make and send a replacement at no extra cost to you.
  • Refund amount. You get back what you paid for the items, after any reward points and coupon. The ₹50 delivery charge is not refunded. Points you used come back to your balance, and points earned on that order are taken off.
  • Bank details. For a refund you must enter your UPI ID, bank account number (twice, to avoid mistakes) and IFSC code. We send the refund to that account, so please check the details carefully. We are not responsible for a refund sent to details you entered wrongly.
  • Sending the item back. We may ask you to send the item back, and will tell you how. Please keep the item and its packaging in the same condition as you received it, apart from the problem you reported. You can follow each step (return requested, approved, item received back, then refund sent or replacement sent) under “Track return”.
  • Timing. We process an approved refund as soon as we reasonably can. It may then take a little time to show in your bank account.
